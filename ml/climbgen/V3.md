# Climb generator v3 (Kilter Original only)

v3 targets climbs that look easy on screen but climb nonsensically. It is live only for
Kilter Original (`kilter-1`), behind a v2/v3 switch on the generator screen. Every other board
stays on v2.

## What changed from v2

| Part | v2 | v3 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Writer | grade + angle + tags | adds a QUALITY token (classic / ordinary), dropped 20% of the time in training |
| Ranking grader | Deep Sets | same Deep Sets grader (the attention grader failed its gate, see below) |
| Realism | none | attention critic: "does this look human-set?" |
| Geometry | none | safety net: reject only beyond the 99th percentile of real classics per grade × angle band |
| Copies | not checked | reject any candidate sharing ≥ 80% of its holds (Jaccard) with a known climb |

"Classic" is a shrunk star average, `(stars × ascents + 2.5 × 10) / (ascents + 10) ≥ 3.0`,
which covers about 35% of climbs.

On the phone, v3 samples candidates at temperature 0.9 (no top-p). It drops geometry outliers
and near-copies, then ranks the rest by `log P_grade(target) + w · max(log σ(critic / T), −5)`
and keeps 6. The settings live in `V3_PIPELINE` in
`packages/mobile/src/lib/climb-generator/generate.ts`.

## Gates and what passed

- **Attention ("pairwise") grader: failed.** Test MAE was 0.98, but range coverage fell to 72%
  at V8–V11 and 53% at V12+, against a target of 80–86%. It is used only as an independent
  judge in `evaluate_v3.py`; nothing in the app uses it.
- **Critic: passed.** AUC 0.794 against a 0.732 baseline built on 15 simple features, with
  calibration temperature 4.15. On real classics against real poorly rated climbs, its AUC is
  0.52. So it spots "machine-made", not "fun".
- **Geometry safety net:** v2 climbs already match real classics on reach and foot support, and
  the net rejects only 1–4% of candidates. The nonsense comes from which holds are combined,
  not from impossible reaches.
- **Top-p 0.9 at temperature 0.85 made geometry worse** (KS 0.16–0.28), so v3 samples at
  temperature 0.9 with no top-p. The critic's negatives were generated at 0.85 / 0.9. The
  sweep below used this mismatched critic, so its numbers stand as measured.

| v3, 48, w 1 | 0.96 | 0.90 | 0.82 | 0.71 | 0.51 | 0.0% |

Selection rule: the largest critic weight whose hit rate stays within 3 points of w = 0 at
every grade. Strictly applied, that rule picks w = 0 (w 0.25 loses 4
points at V7: 0.87 vs 0.91). We shipped w 0.25 anyway. At w 0 the critic does nothing, and it is
the only part aimed at "looks easy, climbs weird". At w 0.25, V7 matches v2 exactly, and the
1-point overshoot is within run-to-run noise.

Against v2, v3 at w 0.25 is level or better at V3–V7, 2 points lower at V9 and 6 lower at V11.
Geometry KS is lower on 2 of 3 features at V7 and V9, but not at V3, V5 or V11; v2 was already
close to real classics. Copies shown are 0%, the geometry net rejects about 3% of candidates, and
variety is no worse than v2. None of these offline numbers measure whether a sequence climbs
well. That is what the v2/v3 switch on the wall is for.

## Hosting

The files live in `fisharmnic/boardsesh-climbgen` under `v3/`, with their own `v3/manifest.json`
(`formatVersion: 2`). The v2 root manifest is untouched. The critic records the writer's
sha256, and the app refuses to pair them if it doesn't match.

## Reproduce (from ml/climbgen/)

Each script's flags are in its own `--help` and module docstring. In order:

```
train.py --model kilter-1 --quality-token     # writer (data/v3-writer.pt, out/v3/kilter-1/)
features.py                                   # out/v3/geometry.json
grader.py --arch attention                    # judge only (data/v3-grader.pt)
critic.py                                     # data/v3-critic.pt, out/v3/kilter-1-critic/
bash out/v3/sweep.sh                          # scorecards (out/v3/score-*.json)
.venv/bin/python -I package_v3.py --version v3-2026-11-07 --grader out/kilter-1/grader.generated.json
.venv/bin/hf upload fisharmnic/boardsesh-climbgen out/hub/v3 v3 --repo-type model
```

## Known limits

The generator has no sequence or beta data, so crosses and body position are still unmodelled.
v3 makes implausible climbs less likely and adds a safety net, but it doesn't simulate a climber.
